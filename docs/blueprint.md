# **App Name**: GulaMonitor

## Core Features:

- Blood Sugar Data Entry: Users can easily input new blood sugar readings, along with date and time, via a dedicated form.
- Custom Range Settings: Users can define and save their personalized healthy blood sugar range (e.g., min and max values).
- Reading History & Display: View a clear, chronological list of all recorded blood sugar readings.
- Out-of-Range Analysis: Calculate and display the percentage of blood sugar readings that fall outside the user-defined healthy range.
- Estimated HbA1c Calculation: Calculate and show an estimated HbA1c value based on the last three months of blood sugar data.
- AI Health Insight Tool: A generative AI tool analyzes blood sugar patterns to offer personalized insights or suggest potential trends.

## Style Guidelines:

- Light color scheme for a clean and professional appearance. The primary color is a calming, trustworthy blue-green (#266BBF), embodying health and clarity. The background is a very light desaturated blue-grey (#EEF3F8), providing a subtle harmony. The accent color is a vibrant cyan (#1FCBDB) to highlight important information and interactive elements.
- The font for all text, including headlines and body, is 'Inter' (sans-serif) for its high readability and modern, objective aesthetic, suitable for data-driven applications.
- Utilize simple, line-based icons to represent health metrics, data input, and settings, ensuring visual clarity and a modern, minimalist feel.
- A clean, intuitive layout with a dashboard-like overview, prioritizing immediate understanding of current blood sugar status and historical data. Ensure full responsiveness across device sizes.
- Implement subtle, non-distracting animations for transitions between screens and feedback during data entry or loading states to enhance user experience without overwhelming it.
